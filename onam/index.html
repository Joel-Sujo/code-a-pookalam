A pookalam, rendered in code

Every Onam, families lay concentric rings of flower petals on the threshold to welcome King Mahabali home. This one is drawn instead of picked — the same rings, the same nested bloom, built entirely out of loops, rotations, and colour.

18 outer leaves
10 petal pairs
700 rim squares
5 core layers
live canvas 1000 × 1000

Rendered live with p5.js — resize the window and the pookalam scales with it, ring for ring.
